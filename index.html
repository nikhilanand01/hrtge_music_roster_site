SRI

Singer / Songwriter / Pop

SRI is a Los Angeles-based musician, songwriter, and content creator. A Berklee-trained vocalist with deep roots in Carnatic music and South Indian vocal traditions, she first captured global attention in 2020 when her cover of “All I Want” by Olivia Rodrigo was met with an outpouring of support. SRI continued to post song covers, amassing over a million followers across platforms.

As she continues to amass her follower base across platforms through her powerful, candid vocals, she’s creating and songwriting every day, developing herself into the star she knows she will be. SRI received a co-sign from Meghan Trainor at age 20 and has performed multiple covers alongside her.

Beyond her social media presence, she has worked on 20+ brand deals including original music for Starbucks & Dove. Recently featured in Vogue India, she is currently collaborating with world-class songwriters and producers on her debut album.

Lil Late

Alternative / Indie / Rock

Lil Late is a rising indie/melancholic artist based in Los Angeles with roots in Dhaka who invites listeners to get lost in his sound. With his debut track “Worth It,” Lil Late instantly captured listeners’ attention, with the accompanying music video being viewed over half a million times. Lil Late followed up with his sophomore release “So Wrong,” gaining major indie support across platforms from tastemakers including Mr. Shades, Aminium Music, and Daniel Mena among others.

His raw and vulnerable artistry has garnered the attention of listeners worldwide and grown a humble and dedicated following. The rising talent’s next single “Call Me?” wrestled with the difficulties of being honest about your feelings. A cinematic exploration of the aftermath of a relationship, Lil Late weaves through the feelings of loss and sadness with a flow all his own.

Mera Bhai

Disco / Global

Karthik Poduval, AKA Mera Bhai, is a London-based producer, DJ, multi-instrumentalist and tastemaker. He is also known as a founding member of psych band Flamingods and former internal team member of Asian Underground collective Daytimers.

Born in London, with roots in India, alongside time spent growing up in Italy, Albania, Saudi Arabia, Dubai and Nigeria, Mera Bhai’s worldly influence seeps into his unique take on dance music.

Rooted in club culture, Mera Bhai leans on UK Garage, French House and 90s rave, whilst taking influence from Indian Carnatic, 70s Brazilian Funk, and Disco & Soul. He is a renowned tastemaker, which is seen and shared widely across his socials.

Hrishi

South Indian Classical / Mixed Language

Hrishi is a composer, vocalist, and storyteller whose work bridges the ancient and the contemporary. Rooted in the rich tradition of Carnatic music and shaped by the global language of R&B and pop, his songs weave lineage into modern sound. A trailblazer within the South Asian diaspora, Hrishi’s work has resonated with millions—earning him a devoted following of over 400,000 listeners, and drawing recognition from artists like John Legend, Camila Cabello, Martin Garrix, and SZA.

Through the visionary work of Hrishi and Raga Prana, Kamakshi introduces the ancient art of sound healing, the divine raga devathas, and the cosmic presence of Devi—the Divine Feminine—to a new generation of listeners.

He also recently launched his music academy, Make Indian Music, an immersive platform that presents a complete framework for people across the world to learn one of the most beautiful music systems in the world, Carnatic music.

Sandunes

Electronic

SANDUNES is the artist project of Sanaya Ardeshir—composer, producer, and sound artist from Mumbai/LA. Her sound is a constant evolution, ranging from experimental piano to cinematic art-pop, seamlessly melding electronic orchestration with live performance.

Sandunes has performed globally at festivals like Lollapalooza and Roskilde, and iconic venues from London’s Jazz Cafe to the Barbican Centre. Her portfolio includes commissions for Warp Records and Boiler Room, US tours supporting Pretty Lights, and opening slots for Bonobo. A recognized cultural force, she has remixed Anoushka Shankar, hosted a BBC Asian Network residency, and recently received the Matt Marks Impact Award.

Beyond the stage, she is a pioneer in spatial audio and sound design, most recently composing for the landmark India in Fashion exhibition at Mumbai’s NMACC. As co-founder of Ears to the Ground, she works at the intersection of art and ecology, further establishing her role as a guest lecturer for Ableton and Apple.

Raashi Kulkarni

Composer / Songwriter

Raashi Kulkarni is a composer and classical pianist based in Los Angeles. She has established a distinct compositional voice that can be heard across a variety of genres, including drama, comedy, adventure, animation, and action. Raashi’s global perspective, study of different music disciplines, and passion for storytelling have been instrumental in the compositions she has developed for a vast spectrum of film and television projects. Her scoring work can be heard in the feature film, A Nice Indian Boy, starring Jonathan Groff and Karan Soni, directed by Roshan Sethi, the Netflix and Imagine Entertainment feature, Wedding Season, directed by Tom Dey, the award-winning documentary, Barefoot Empress, written and directed by Michelin Star Chef Vikas Khanna, the Disney+ film, World’s Best, from director Roshan Sethi and producers Thomas Kail and Utkarsh Ambudkar, and the NAACP Image Award nominated documentary, ifine: beauty. Kulkarni is the 2023 recipient of The ASCAP Foundation David Rose Award and a 2024 ASCAP Composers’ Choice Award Nominee for Film Score of the Year (World’s Best). PINCH, Raashi’s latest feature film, had its world premiere at Tribeca Film Festival under the International Narrative Competition. Her most recent collaboration with DreamWorks Animation, Wednesdays with Gramps, premiered at the American Black Film Festival, went on to screen at dozens of festivals worldwide, and garnered multiple honors, including an NAACP Award. Its score earned her a Hollywood Music in Media nomination. She also earned a 2025 SCL David Raksin Award for Emerging Talent nomination and an ASCAP 2026 Composers’ Choice nomination for Film Score of the Year (A Nice Indian Boy).

In addition to film and television scoring, Raashi is a globally recognized musical artist. Her two independent music albums, Raashi (2018) and Afterglow (2020), debuted at #1 and #2, respectively, on the iTunes World Music Charts. Kulkarni actively contributes to the community as a current Governor and former Advisor on the Recording Academy’s LA Chapter Board, a GRAMMY U Mentor, a Board of Directors member and former Executive Director of the Alliance for Women Film Composers, and as a participant in the prestigious Universal Composers Initiative (2021-2023). Raashi was one of eight composers and the first person of Indian origin to be selected. The two year program was developed by NBCUniversal’s Global Talent Development & Inclusion group partnered with Universal Global Film Music (Hollywood Reporter).

Over the last decade, Raashi has performed in concert tours across the globe with several prominent artists from the United States and India, including world-renowned Bollywood composers, Vishal Dadlani and Shekhar Ravjiani. She has performed as a featured pianist at the Shrine Auditorium, Kennedy Center, Smithsonian Museum, Patriot Center, Warner Theatre, and Kodak Hall. Her YouTube piano covers, which are arranged solely by ear, have been discovered and recognized by iHeartRadio, On Air with Ryan Seacrest, Capitol Records (as part of Beck’s successful GRAMMY campaign), YouTube India, and Pharrell Williams’ partnership with The United Nations Foundation.

Kahme

Hip-Hop

KAHME is a New York-based hip-hop artist known for his confident delivery, cinematic production choices, and ability to create high-energy records that connect across rooms and cultures.

With hundreds of thousands of streams on Spotify, Kahme has steadily built momentum through a growing catalog that balances bangers with intention, positioning him as a distinctive voice in modern rap.

Drawing subtle influence from the melodies and cultural rhythm of his Punjabi background, Kahme brings a unique texture to his sound without being confined by genre or identity.

As he continues to expand his audience and sharpen his artistic world, Kahme is focused on consistency, growth, and longevity—building a body of work that feels essential, memorable, and built to last.

Peter Madana

Hip-Hop / Producer / Artist

Peter Madana, originally from Chicago, IL and based in Los Angeles, CA, is a mastering engineer, record producer, sound designer, and songwriter. He hails the audio world with modern, contemporary, and classical fusion musical elements. His passion for the art of audio drives his persistence to an unimaginable correlation of space & time.

With an education background of audio engineering, Madana’s passion for audio has created an eclectic clientele base, including artists such as Jay Sean, DJ Flipside, Raghav, Fabulous, Raja Kumari, Omarion, and Ty Dolla $ign.
